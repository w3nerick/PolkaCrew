# PolkaCrew visual direction: Neon Orbital

This prompt sheet keeps future image generations in the same original visual family.

## Core language

- Polished 2D game art with clean vector-like cartoon rendering.
- Crisp graphite outlines, soft cel shading and readable silhouettes.
- Near-black plum, graphite, pearl white, Polkadot magenta, violet and cyan.
- Faceted helmets, separate magnetic boots, hexagonal relay modules and network-node light motifs.
- Friendly sci-fi with a suspicious social-deduction undertone.
- Never copy copyrighted characters, maps or the rounded bean silhouette associated with Among Us.

## Relay Ranger

Create one original PolkaCrew crew member: a compact futuristic space technician with a faceted circular helmet, cyan wraparound visor, short angular torso, two separated magnetic boots, articulated arms and a hexagonal relay battery behind one shoulder. Add a small glowing magenta network-node emblem and subtle violet edge lights. Isolate the full-body three-quarter character on genuine alpha transparency; no text, floor, shadow or watermark.

## Chain Mechanic

Create a cheerful heavy-duty ship engineer with reinforced angular shoulders, tool gauntlets, a circular chain-link diagnostic backpack, amber work lights and a broad cyan visor. Use graphite and pearl-white armor, warm orange highlights and restrained magenta network lights. Preserve the Relay Ranger proportions and genuine alpha transparency.

## Bulletin Diver

Create a nimble deep-data recovery specialist with a pressure-sealed helmet, twin translucent cyan data canisters, fin-like antenna tabs and lightweight forearm scanners. Use navy and pearl-white armor, cyan and cobalt highlights and small magenta nodes. Preserve the Relay Ranger proportions and genuine alpha transparency.

## Validator Warden

Create a calm network-security specialist with a hexagonal chest shield emitter, symmetric signal antennae, layered forearms and a compact shield-projector backpack. Use pearl white, graphite, emerald signal lights, cyan glass and small violet-magenta accents. Preserve the Relay Ranger proportions and genuine alpha transparency.

## Orbit Medic

Create a friendly zero-gravity rescue specialist with a circular wrist scanner, integrated medical storage capsules and an abstract waveform made only from glowing bars. Avoid a red-cross symbol. Use pearl white, graphite, rose-magenta, lavender, cyan and small mint status lights. Preserve the Relay Ranger proportions and genuine alpha transparency.

## Fork Wraith

Create one original PolkaCrew saboteur in the same proportions and rendering family as the Relay Ranger. Use an angular stealth exosuit, fractured asymmetrical helmet, narrow hot-red visor split by a glitch crack, magnetic boots, maintenance claws and a damaged relay battery leaking restrained magenta-violet data particles. Ominous but not gruesome. Isolate on genuine alpha transparency; no text, floor, shadow or watermark.

## Relay Beacon

Create one compact floating hexagonal task terminal with a recessed cyan core, four network nodes connected by glowing lines, two stabilizer feet, graphite and pearl panels and Polkadot-magenta accents. Slight top-down three-quarter view, readable at 48–72 px, isolated on genuine alpha transparency. No words, logo, shadow or watermark.

## Relay Ark

Create a 16:10 top-down open spaceship deck for a 1000×620 game Canvas. Place a circular emergency-consensus platform at the center and four distinct task zones near the corners, joined by broad unobstructed paths. Use dark plum floor panels, graphite framing, violet conduits, magenta nodes, cyan window light and outer views of deep space. No characters, text, logo, UI or copyrighted map layout.

## Runtime rules

- Every player receives a deterministic crew skin based on their player id; cosmetic variety never reveals roles.
- The Fork Wraith appears only for the local player when their assigned role is saboteur.
- Player colors appear as identity rings and pips instead of recoloring the detailed artwork.
- Keep future sprites transparent and environment art at a 16:10 ratio.
